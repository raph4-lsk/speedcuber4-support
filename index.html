SpeedCuber4 is a free speedcubing timer for iPhone and iPad: WCA random state scrambles, 15 second inspection, ao5, ao12 and ao100, trend and distribution charts, GAN Smart Timer over Bluetooth, and optional sync of your sessions through your own Google Drive.

Your data

Your solves and settings stay on your device. SpeedCuber4 has no server; if you turn on sync, your sessions are saved only in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use.
