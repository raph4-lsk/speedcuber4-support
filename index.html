Help and support

How to use SpeedCuber4, and how to send feedback.

Gestures

Start a solve
Hold anywhere on the timer until the digits turn green, then let go.
Stop a solve
Touch anywhere on the screen.
Inspection
With inspection on, tap once to start the 15 second WCA inspection, then hold and let go to start.
New scramble
Swipe right on the timer.
Delete the last solve
Swipe left on the timer, then tap Undo within a few seconds to bring it back.
Large scramble net
Tap the cube net next to the quick stats.
Solve detail
Tap a time in Times to see its scramble, penalty and comment.
Delete a time
Touch and hold a time in Times.
Average detail
Tap an average in Stats to see its solves.
Explore the trend
Drag a finger along the ao12 chart in Stats.
Manage a session
Tap the ••• button of a session to rename, export or delete it.

Import and export

From the Sessions screen, export a session as CSV (opens in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets), as csTimer CSV, or as a SpeedCuber4 JSON backup. Import reads SpeedCuber4 JSON, csTimer exports and CSV files with a Time column. An import always creates new sessions and never changes the ones you have.

Your data

Your solves and settings stay on your iPhone. SpeedCuber4 has no account and sends nothing anywhere. Read the privacy policy.

Feedback

Your feedback shapes the next versions. Tell what you like, what is missing, or what gets in the way of your practice. In the app, Settings › Send feedback opens the form with your app version and iPhone filled in. A free GitHub account is needed.

Send feedback

Report a bug

Something does not work? Describe what you did and what you saw, with your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version from Settings › About.
