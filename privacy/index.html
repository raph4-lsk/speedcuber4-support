Privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026

SpeedCuber4 does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your sessions, solves, times, scrambles, comments and settings are saved in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are included in your iPhone or iCloud backups according to your iOS settings, under Apple's privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No account and no sign in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third party services that collect data.
  • No network requests with your data: the app works fully offline.

Files you import or export

Import only reads the file you choose in the file picker. Export creates a file and opens the iOS share sheet; the file goes only where you send it.

Voice alerts and clipboard

Inspection voice alerts are spoken by iOS on your device. The app writes to the clipboard only when you tap Copy.

Deleting your data

Delete a solve or a session in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
